NotesGraph AI

AI-Powered Lecture Notes and Chat Assistant

Hover Over the image to see image description

NotesGraph AI is a full-stack AI application that turns long lecture recordings into structured study notes and a searchable knowledge base. It covers the complete pipeline, from resumable audio upload to transcription, note generation, vector retrieval and chat answers grounded in the lecture itself.


Key Functionalities:

  • Secure sign-up and sign-in with Supabase Auth; lectures, notes, chats and audio are owner-scoped using row-level security and a private storage bucket.
  • Resumable audio uploads (up to 500 MB) straight to private storage, with transcription and note generation running in the background.
  • Transcription with Groq Whisper, keeping segment-level timestamps across the whole recording.
  • Structured notes generated by Gemini, with a Groq fallback when Gemini is temporarily unavailable.
  • RAG-based chat powered by Supabase pgvector; answers point to the matching moment in the recording.
  • Honest answers: if a question is not covered in the lecture, the assistant says so instead of inventing references.
  • Light and dark themes, packaged as a Docker image and deployed on Render.

Library Dashboard:

  • Lectures grouped into subject folders that are reused across your account.
  • Status cards show whether each lecture is uploading, processing, ready or failed, with a retry option.
  • Blank titles are generated automatically from the lecture's topic.

Lecture Page:

  • Structured study notes alongside the lecture audio and transcript.
  • Chat history saved per lecture, so you can pick a conversation back up.
  • Timestamp chips that take you straight to the relevant part of the recording.
